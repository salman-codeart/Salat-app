Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Short version: this app collects nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party trackers, no servers. Everything runs on your phone.

1. Information we collect

None. Salat-সালাত does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. There is no sign-in, no user account and no backend server.

2. Location

If you tap “My location”, the app asks Android for your device’s approximate location and uses it only on the device to work out your nearest district among the 64 districts of Bangladesh. The coordinates are never sent anywhere and are not saved after the app closes. Granting location permission is optional — you can always pick a district from the list instead.

3. Data stored on your device

The following stays in your phone’s local storage (and is removed when you uninstall the app):

  • Selected district and calculation method
  • Language and theme preference
  • Prayer tracker entries, qada counts and dhikr counts
  • Notification settings and bookmarked hadith

4. Internet

The app works fully offline. Prayer times, the Hijri date, the qibla bearing and the Ramadan calendar are calculated on your device. No prayer-time or location data is downloaded or uploaded.

Since version 2.4 the app makes one optional request: when you open it, it downloads a small public version.json file from the project’s GitHub page to check whether a newer version exists. That request contains no personal data — only the file address, the same way any web page is fetched. Nothing about you, your location, your prayers or your usage is included. If you are offline the request simply fails and the app continues offline as usual.

5. Notifications and alarms

Waqt reminders use Android’s AlarmManager and are scheduled on the device. You can turn them off at any time inside the app or in Android settings.

6. Third parties

The app contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK. No data is shared with anyone because no data leaves your device.

7. Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same URL with a new date.
